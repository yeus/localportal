## Exercise 1: Problem Analysis and Solution Design

### Objective
Apply the problem-solving framework from the "Getting Started" module to analyze and solve a structured problem.

### Scenario
You've been tasked with designing a system to manage student enrollment for a university. The system must handle:

- Student registration and profile management
- Course catalog and scheduling
- Enrollment limits and prerequisites
- Payment processing and financial aid
- Reporting and analytics

### Your Task

1. **Problem Identification**
   - List all stakeholders and their needs
   - Identify constraints and requirements
   - Define success criteria

2. **Research and Analysis**
   - What existing solutions exist?
   - What are the industry best practices?
   - What technologies would be appropriate?

3. **Solution Design**
   - Create a high-level architecture
   - Define key components and their interactions
   - Plan for scalability and maintenance
